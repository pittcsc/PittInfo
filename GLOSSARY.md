# PittInfo

Campus availability information for Pitt students.

## Language

**Campus availability**:
Information students use to decide where to go or which campus resource to use, including dining, exercise facilities, study spaces, computing labs, and shuttles.

**Study-space availability**:
Building opening hours and the availability of rooms that students can reserve; this does not include estimates of empty seats.

**Favorite location**:
A campus location a student saves for quick access on the same browser, without an account or synchronization across devices.
